Treatment Recommendations & Plan
Neem oil spray
Organic Certified
Apply cold-pressed Neem Oil emulsion (5ml per 1L of water with 1ml liquid soap). Spray thoroughly during early morning or evening hours every 3 days to suffocate spores and reduce fungal spread.
Frequency: Every 3 Days • Dilution: 5ml / 1L Water • Safety Rating: High (Non-toxic to bees)
Copper fungicide
Fast Acting
Apply Copper Hydroxide or Copper Oxychloride spray (2g per 1L water) every 5–7 days. Copper acts as a protective barrier on leaf tissue preventing germ tube penetration. Observe a 1-day harvest interval after application.
Frequency: Every 5–7 Days • Dosage: 2g / 1L Water • Harvest Delay: 24 Hours
Trichoderma Bio-Control & Canopy Pruning
Eco Restoration
Perform soil root drenching with Trichoderma harzianum bio-fungicide to colonize roots. Prune bottom 30cm of lower leaves with dark brown spots to stop soil-splash spore inoculation.
Application: Root Drench & Leaf Pruning • Effect: Root Shielding
